## Page 04 — INNOVATION POINTS

INNOVATION POINTS

1.  
Map enhancement: Make features on the map have listening and speaking capabilities,Like physical stores, buildings, firms, schools, etc.,  
even a tree or a pond can be revitalized.  
2.  
Interactive Street: Walking through the streets, information comes to you.  
3.  
Self-aggregating network: Stores automatically connect with customers.  
4.  
Equal Rights Network: The flaw in a follower network is that it is unfair. It’s hard for an ordinary person to have as large a network of followers  
as an influencer. A network should be built so that messages from anyone can reach everyone (messages are received in digest form by  
default in the user's public area, unless the user is interested in this source of the message)  
5.  
Financial innovation: Combining blockchain with physical stores to create a new type of financial infrastructure to solve the problem of  
financing difficulties for small and medium-sized firms, and at the same time provide ordinary people with a new financing channel, moreover  
engage in no feeling financing, so that ordinary people can also share the benefits of small and medium-sized firms development dividends,  
just like its shareholders. In addition, finance is combined with geography for the first time, allowing financial flowers to bloom on the earth.  
From then on, geographical finance will become a new field.  
6.  
Geographic traffic and content pool: The world is organized into traffic pools by country and administrative regions at all levels, with streets (or  
villages) as the basic traffic pool, ranging from small to large and even global traffic pools. Keep content and traffic distributed geographically.  
Each level of traffic pool has a review and recommendation mechanism as well as a fair and open policy.  
7.  
Augmented reality games: combined with geography, allow users to enjoy the fun of interacting with geography. For example, Raise  
geographical AI creatures(GeoAICreatures), Wherever you go, the AI ​​creatures will follow you in augmented reality; Money Fountain is a  
geography game with a geographical sensing range. Combined with geo-finance, It can continuously sprinkle money into the sensing range at  
a certain speed, and the closer the user is to the fountain, the higher the weight he will get, the faster you earn money. Other geography  
games include Navigation to grab red envelopes on the map, Navigation to grab treasures on the map, etc.

Others：

1.  
Asset appreciation: Automatically attract  
followers for merchants: By scanning QR  
codes to pay, entering the geographical  
range of the store, likes, comments, etc.  
Merchants can push information or services  
to these followers at any time. Followers are  
the value, and their value will increase with  
the time keeps accumulating

2.  
Sustainable incentive mechanism: It is a connection  
tool based on geographical finance and a bridge  
between interactive subjects and users. This  
mechanism uses Lucky Cat to condense incentives for  
users, such as likes, comments, within the  
geographical range, etc.  Lucky Cat will automatically  
connect to these users, and it will continuously issue  
incentives to these users. Lucky Cat is also an engine  
that can make money while walking.

3.  
Fission Game: Geochat has extraordinary  
innovation in attracting traffic. It uses  
telegram, whatsapp, paypal, WeChat,  
tiktok, mobile contacts and other media to  
directly attract customers to group chats,  
official broadcasts or official ARealities,  
automatically attract followers. Moreover, C-  
end users can also profit from it, which is  
very interesting.
